## 🌍 My Mission as a Developer

> I believe the best software is the kind that **disappears** — where the user only thinks about their goal, never the interface.

I build with three things in mind: **people**, **performance**, and **purpose.**  
Whether it's a healthcare platform that needs to be trusted, or an open-source tool that needs to be fast — I care about the *why* behind every line of code.

I'm not just chasing frameworks or trends. I'm working toward being the kind of engineer who can look at a hard problem, break it down, and ship something that actually matters.
